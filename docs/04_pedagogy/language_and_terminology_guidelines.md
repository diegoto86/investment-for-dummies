# Language & Financial Terminology Guidelines

**Status:** Canonical  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Project:** Invest Smarter with AI  
**Recommended path:** `docs/04_pedagogy/language_and_terminology_guidelines.md`  
**Domain Authority:** Language conventions for courses, slides, templates, examples, and student-facing product UI.

---

# 1. Purpose

This document defines how language and financial terminology should be used across the learning experience.

The program is taught primarily in **Spanish**, but students should learn the **English financial terminology they will encounter in real investing platforms, market-data websites, company filings, earnings materials, and research tools**.

The objective is not to translate the financial world into artificial Spanish terminology.

The objective is to help Spanish-speaking students understand the concepts while becoming fluent in the vocabulary used by the market.

---

# 2. Core Rule

> **Financial concept names stay in English. Explanations stay in Spanish.**

Examples:

**Free Cash Flow (FCF)**  
Caja que genera el negocio después de cubrir sus necesidades operativas y de inversión en capital.

**Market Cap**  
Valor de mercado de todas las acciones de una compañía.

**Drawdown**  
Caída desde un máximo previo hasta un mínimo posterior.

**Margin of Safety**  
Colchón entre el precio actual y un rango de valoración razonable, considerando incertidumbre y downside.

---

# 3. First-Mention Rule

The first time an important term appears in a course, lesson, slide, template, or interface:

Use:

**English Term (Acronym, when relevant)** — explicación clara en español.

Example:

**Free Cash Flow (FCF)** — caja generada por el negocio después de cubrir CapEx.

After the term has been introduced, prefer:

**FCF**

or:

**Free Cash Flow**

Do not repeatedly translate it.

---

# 4. Canonical Term Rule

Each financial concept should have one preferred canonical English term.

Avoid switching unnecessarily between synonyms.

Example:

Preferred:

**Revenue**

Avoid alternating between:

- Revenue
- Sales
- Ventas
- Ingresos

unless the distinction is relevant.

Spanish may be used in the explanation:

> Revenue representa los ingresos generados por la actividad del negocio.

# 14. Spanish Narrative

The following should normally be written in Spanish:

- conceptual explanations;
- teaching narrative;
- slide headlines when the headline is not itself a financial term;
- instructions;
- exercises;
- questions;
- examples;
- warnings;
- conclusions;
- student feedback;
- navigation;
- course descriptions;
- help text.

Example:

> **¿Por qué puede caer una buena empresa después de buenos Earnings?**

This is preferable to artificially translating **Earnings**.

---

# 16. Mixed-Language Headlines

Mixed Spanish/English is acceptable when it mirrors how an investor naturally communicates.

Examples:

> ¿Qué nos dice realmente el Free Cash Flow?

> Revenue crece, pero ¿está creciendo también el EPS?

> Una caída del 30% no crea automáticamente un Margin of Safety.

> Market Order vs Limit Order: ¿cuándo usar cada una?

Avoid forced Spanglish when a normal Spanish sentence is clearer.

---

# 17. Translation Decision Rule

Before translating a financial term, ask:

> ¿El alumno encontrará este término principalmente en inglés cuando utilice herramientas reales?

If yes:

**Keep it in English.**

If the term is normal explanatory language rather than market vocabulary:

**Use Spanish.**

---

# 18. Acronym Rule

Use the internationally recognized acronym.

Examples:

- EPS
- FCF
- EBITDA
- ROIC
- RSI
- TER
- AUM
- DCA
- CAGR

Do not create Spanish acronyms.

On first use:

**Earnings Per Share (EPS)** — beneficio atribuible por acción.

Then:

**EPS**

---

# 19. Numbers and Units

Use the presentation format most appropriate for the target market and data source.

When reproducing financial information:

- preserve the source currency;
- clearly identify currency;
- use standard market units when useful.

Examples:

- $2.4B
- €750M
- 18.2x P/E
- 4.1% FCF Yield
- +23% Upside

Spanish explanatory text may use:

- millones;
- miles de millones;

but tables and market-data cards may retain:

- M
- B

when visually clearer.

# 20. Company Filings and Source Material

Do not translate official document names unnecessarily.

Use:

- Annual Report
- 10-K
- 10-Q
- Earnings Release
- Earnings Call
- Investor Presentation
- Investor Relations
- Guidance

Then explain their function in Spanish.

Example:

**10-K** — informe anual regulatorio presentado por compañías estadounidenses ante la SEC.

# 21. Consistency Across Courses

Once a term is introduced, use the same term throughout courses 000–501.

Examples:

Always prefer:

- Revenue
- Free Cash Flow
- Competitive Advantage
- Margin of Safety
- Investment Thesis
- Thesis Invalidation
- Position Sizing

Do not switch back and forth between translated variants.

---

# 22. Consistency Across Artifacts

Templates should use the same English terms students learned in the lessons.

Example:

Course:

**Thesis Invalidation**

Template:

**Thesis Invalidation**

Not:

**Condición de invalidación de tesis**

The field can include Spanish help text.

---

# 23. Consistency Across the Web

The platform, slides, templates, charts, and exercises should share one vocabulary.

A student should not learn:

**Free Cash Flow**

in a lesson and later see:

**Flujo de Caja Libre**

as the primary label in the application.

The canonical term remains:

**Free Cash Flow**

---

# 24. Avoid Unnecessary Jargon

Keeping financial terminology in English does not mean maximizing jargon.

Do not introduce technical terms merely because professionals use them.

Every term should answer:

> ¿Necesita el alumno reconocer o utilizar este término para tomar mejores decisiones o navegar herramientas reales?

If not, omit it.

---

# 25. Teaching Pattern

Recommended pattern for a new concept:

## Term

**Free Cash Flow (FCF)**

## Explanation

Es la caja que genera la empresa después de financiar su operación y las inversiones necesarias en activos.

## Why It Matters

Nos ayuda a entender cuánta caja económica queda disponible después de mantener el negocio.

## Where You Will See It

- company filings;
- Yahoo Finance;
- Koyfin;
- Finviz;
- valuation tools;
- earnings analysis.

## Decision

¿Está creciendo el FCF de manera sostenible?

# 26. Example Slide Language

Preferred:

# Revenue está creciendo. ¿Está mejorando realmente el negocio?

Una empresa puede aumentar Revenue sin mejorar sus economics.

Revisaremos:

- Gross Margin
- Operating Margin
- EPS
- Free Cash Flow

Avoid replacing the canonical labels with Spanish translations as the primary terminology.

---

# 27. Language North Star

The student should finish the program able to:

- understand financial concepts in Spanish;
- recognize their standard English terminology instantly;
- navigate brokers and research platforms without translation friction;
- read company materials with progressively less dependence on translation;
- communicate using the vocabulary used by investors and markets.

The guiding principle is:

> **Teach in Spanish. Speak the language of the market.**
